# Demo Inputs — Future-Self Letter Generator

Ten realistic user profiles you can copy-paste into the form at `/generate`.
Each entry maps 1:1 to the form fields. Fields marked *(optional)* can be
left blank if you prefer.

---

## 1. The Career Switcher (32, quitting a stable job)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Priya

**What's going on in your life right now?**
I'm 32 and I've spent eight years in accounting. I'm good at it, the pay is fine, but every Sunday night I feel a quiet dread. I've been learning UX design for a year — late nights, weekends — and I just got a junior offer that pays 40% less than what I make now. My parents think I'm being reckless. My savings will cover about ten months if things go wrong.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That I'm too old to start over and I'll be competing with 24-year-olds. That the pay cut will set me back so far I'll never catch up. That in two years I'll discover design was just a fantasy I liked from the outside.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
To do work that makes me lose track of time. To be a senior designer within four years. To stop measuring my life in billable hours.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Do I accept the junior UX offer next week, or stay one more year and save more first?

**Time horizon**: 5 years
**Tone**: Honest & Realistic

## 2. The New Parent (sleep-deprived, career on pause)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Marcus

**What's going on in your life right now?**
Our daughter is four months old and I haven't slept more than three hours straight since she was born. I love her more than I thought possible, but I also miss who I used to be. My wife went back to work last month and I'm on extended leave. My manager says my role is "safe" but a reorg is coming and I can read between the lines. Money is tighter than we ever admit to our friends.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That my career quietly dies while I'm changing diapers. That my marriage is turning into a logistics operation — we haven't had a real conversation in weeks. That I'll resent this season instead of remembering it fondly.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
To be a present dad, not just a tired one. To come back to work with something to offer, maybe even in a better role. To feel like my wife and I are teammates again.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to extend my leave by three more months or go back early to protect my position before the reorg.

**Time horizon**: 5 years
**Tone**: Warm & Encouraging

## 3. The Burned-Out Founder (startup running out of runway)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Dani

**What's going on in your life right now?**
I started my company three years ago. We have real customers and about $18k in monthly revenue, but growth has been flat for six months and we have nine months of runway left. I've stopped paying myself. Two investors passed last week with the same polite feedback. My co-founder and I argue about direction almost daily now. I'm 29 and I feel 45.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That I'll shut it down and spend the rest of my life wondering if one more pivot would have worked. That I've burned three years and my best friendship for nothing. That taking a normal job afterward will feel like a public admission of failure.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
Either to find the turn that makes this company work, or to walk away clean and proud of what we built. To repair things with my co-founder either way. To remember what a weekend feels like.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to attempt one final pivot with the remaining runway or start an orderly wind-down now while we can still pay everyone.

**Time horizon**: 5 years
**Tone**: Wise & Grounded

## 4. The Fresh Graduate (no job, drowning in comparison)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Tomi

**What's going on in your life right now?**
I graduated eight months ago with a decent degree and I'm still unemployed. I've sent out over 200 applications and had four interviews. I moved back in with my parents, which I'm grateful for and ashamed of at the same time. My university friends are posting about promotions and grad school acceptances. I deliver groceries part-time and tell people it's "temporary."

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That the gap on my CV is becoming the reason for the gap on my CV. That I peaked in university. That my parents' patience — and mine — will run out at the same time.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
A real full-time job in my field, even an unglamorous one. My own apartment, even a small one. To stop flinching when someone asks "so what do you do?"

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to take an unpaid three-month internship that might lead to something, or keep the paid grocery job and keep applying.

**Time horizon**: 5 years
**Tone**: Warm & Encouraging

## 5. The Recently Divorced (42, starting over)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Sarah

**What's going on in your life right now?**
My divorce was finalized two months ago after a 14-year marriage. It was mostly amicable, which somehow makes it lonelier. We're co-parenting two kids, 8 and 11, and I have them every other week. The house feels enormous on the empty weeks. I'm 42, dating apps terrify me, and I just realized I don't actually know what I like to do on a Saturday — I only know what we used to do.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That I'll be alone for the rest of my life. That the kids are more damaged by this than they're showing. That I wasted my "good years" and everything from here is just managing decline.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
To build a life that's genuinely mine, not a smaller copy of my married one. To be the steady parent my kids need. Maybe, eventually, to love someone again without flinching.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to sell the house and move closer to my sister, which would mean changing the kids' school.

**Time horizon**: 10 years
**Tone**: Loving & Soft

## 6. The Immigrant (far from home, building from zero)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Amara

**What's going on in your life right now?**
I moved to a new country eleven months ago for work. My qualifications from home count for less here, so I took a job two levels below what I did before. I send money back to my family every month. My English is good but in meetings I rehearse sentences in my head and stay quiet. I video call my mother every Sunday and lie a little about how well things are going.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That I'll always be seen as "the foreigner who works hard" and never just as a colleague. That my parents will grow old and need me while I'm stuck here proving myself. That I gave up my community back home for a bank account.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
Permanent residency. A role that matches my actual ability. A group of friends here who know the real me, not the careful, polite version.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to spend my savings on a local certification that would validate my credentials, or keep sending that money home.

**Time horizon**: 10 years
**Tone**: Wise & Grounded

## 7. The Laid-Off Tech Worker (mortgage, kids, sudden freefall)

**Your name** *(optional)*: James

**What's going on in your life right now?**
I got laid off three weeks ago after nine years at the same company — a Tuesday morning calendar invite titled "Quick sync." I'm 38 with a mortgage, two kids, and a severance package that runs out in four months. The job market in my specialty is brutal right now; roles that had 30 applicants two years ago have 800. I haven't told my parents yet. I check LinkedIn like it's a slot machine.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That my skills are tied to one company's internal tools and I'm less employable than I thought. That we'll have to sell the house and uproot the kids. That my confidence won't survive months of automated rejection emails.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
To land somewhere I can stay for years, not just anywhere that will have me. To use this forced pause to figure out if I even want the same career. To someday describe this as "the layoff that ended up being a good thing."

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to hold out for a senior role in my field or take a contract gig now to stop the savings bleed.

**Time horizon**: 5 years
**Tone**: Honest & Realistic

## 8. The Health Wake-Up Call (recovering and re-prioritizing)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Elena

**What's going on in your life right now?**
Six weeks ago I collapsed at work — panic attack plus blood pressure that scared the ER doctor. I'm 45, I run a team of twelve, and I've treated my body like a rental car for two decades. The doctor used the phrase "warning shot." I've started walking every morning and I've had actual dinners with my family, and part of me is already itching to check email during them. My identity is so tangled up in being the reliable one.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That I'll slide back into old patterns the moment the fear fades — I've seen myself do it before. That slowing down means becoming irrelevant at work. That the next warning won't be a warning.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
To be healthy at 55 and not just "managing conditions." To learn to be valuable without being exhausted. To have my kids remember me as present, not as a closed office door.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to ask for a smaller role at work, which feels like career suicide but might be life insurance.

**Time horizon**: 10 years
**Tone**: Loving & Soft

## 9. The Caregiver (30s, life on hold for a parent)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Rosa

**What's going on in your life right now?**
My mother was diagnosed with early-stage Alzheimer's last year, and as the only sibling in the same city, her care has landed on me. I'm 34. I moved into her spare room "temporarily" eight months ago. I work remotely and mute calls when she gets confused. My brother sends money and advice from abroad; neither is what I actually need. I broke up with someone good because I had nothing left to give. Some days she calls me by her sister's name.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That I'll disappear into this role and emerge in my forties with no career, no partner, and no idea who I am. That I'll resent her, and then hate myself for the resentment. That I'm doing all of this and it still won't be enough.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
To care for her well without erasing myself. To get real help — professional help — without feeling like I'm abandoning her. To still be building some kind of life of my own alongside this.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to move her into an assisted living facility next year, which she made me promise never to do back when she could still make promises.

**Time horizon**: 5 years
**Tone**: Loving & Soft

## 10. The Quiet Dreamer (safe job, unwritten novel)

**Your name** *(optional)*: Dev

**What's going on in your life right now?**
I'm 27 and I work in insurance. It's fine. That's the problem — everything is fine. I've been writing a novel in a notes app for three years; it's 60,000 words of fragments that nobody has ever read. Every January I promise myself this is the year I take writing seriously, and every December I've somehow written less than the year before. My friends know me as the practical one. I've never told anyone I want to be a writer out loud.

**Your biggest fears or worries**
That I'll show someone my writing and finally get confirmation that it's mediocre. That "aspiring writer" quietly becomes "guy who used to write" without me noticing. That fine is all my life will ever be, and that I chose it.

**Your goals or what you hope for**
To finish one complete draft, even a bad one. To submit something, anywhere, and survive the rejection. To find out at 27 rather than 47 whether there's anything real in me.

**A specific decision you're facing** *(optional)*
Whether to join a local writers' workshop next month, which means reading my work aloud to strangers.

**Time horizon**: 10 years
**Tone**: Honest & Realistic
